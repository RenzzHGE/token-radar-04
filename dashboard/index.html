How radar-4 scores

Every row is scored by one function from the snapshot's own figures. No protocol name is special-cased.

  1. Base, log scale: √TVL × 3000. A protocol 100× larger in TVL is worth 10× more, not 100×.
  2. Growth: 1 + clamp(7-day change, −60%, +120%) ÷ 100 × 0.40.
  3. Chain pay: min(1.35, 1 + 0.70 × summed chain weight). A chain's weight is its share of the live set's total TVL, each protocol's TVL split evenly across its chains.
  4. Crowding: 1 ÷ (1 + 0.06 × ln(n)), n the rankable protocols in the category. Crowded categories pay less.
  5. Bleed: when the 7-day drop passes −20%, up to a further 15% off at −40% and beyond, on top of the growth term.
  6. Completeness haircut: −3% when the 7-day change is missing, −2% when the listing date is unreported.
  7. Maturity: 0.85 + 0.15 × min(age in days, 730) ÷ 730. Protocols that ran longer with no token score higher.

Implied value = base × growth × chain pay × crowding × bleed × haircut × maturity, rounded to whole dollars.
